My story

From taking apart the family TV to building training data for AI.

  1. 01

    Taking things apart

    I grew up in China, and I grew up loving computers and electronics. I would take apart my parents’ TV to see how it worked, then put it back together.

  2. 02

    Games, poker and the market

    I spent a lot of my childhood playing League of Legends and Dota, and I still play poker in my spare time. In middle school I started trading stocks, and I made the first $10,000 of my life in the market.

  3. 03

    Sustain Globe Unity

    I care a lot about protecting the environment. At 16 I founded Sustain Globe Unity, a nonprofit that grew past 100 members, raised more than $20,000 and partnered with the United Nations.

    • 100+members
    • $20k+raised
    • UNpartner
  4. 04

    Research at Rice

    At Rice I study computer science. I did research on NLP reliability with Dr. Hanjie Chen, and then joined Dr. Eugene Ng to work on network checkpointing in datacenters. The biggest thing both taught me is that data is everything.

“Data is everything.”
  1. 05

    Post-training

    With that in mind, I started learning reinforcement learning and doing independent research on RL and post-training. I came away convinced that post-training is what decides whether a model is good or not.

  2. 06

    Why I started SoftMax

    Research showed me that post-training and data are the two things that will matter most in the years ahead. That’s why I started SoftMax.

What I’m building now

SoftMax builds long-horizon training environments from real business histories.

Companies license records they no longer use, and AI labs train agents on months of real work instead of invented tasks.
