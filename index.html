My story

I grew up loving computers and electronics. I used to take apart my parents’ TV and put it back together. I played a lot of League of Legends and Dota, and I still play poker in my spare time.

In middle school I started trading stocks, and I made the first $1,000 of my life in the market. I also cared about protecting the environment, so at 16 I founded Sustain Globe Unity, a nonprofit that grew to more than 100 members, raised over $20,000 and partnered with the United Nations.

At Rice, where I study computer science, I built a website to help Rice students find parties. This was before Claude Code, so I hand-coded the whole thing myself 😤. I wanted to turn it into a startup and scale it, but it didn’t work out, so it stayed a project. Next I started Project David, a startup that connected smart home devices with AI agents, a kind of Jarvis that could control everything in your home. That failed too.

After that, I turned to research. I worked on NLP reliability with Dr. Hanjie Chen, then joined Dr. Eugene Ng to research network checkpointing in datacenters. The biggest thing I learned from both is that data is everything.

With that in mind, I started learning reinforcement learning and doing independent research on RL and post-training. I came away convinced that post-training decides whether a model is good or not, and that post-training and data are the two things that will matter most. That’s why I started SoftMax.

I love startups and I've always wanted to do startups for life. I think the reason I am so passionate about startups is because I am neurodivergent. (aka I hate boring work and being an npc.)

I am passionate about physics, and I can yap about it whole day. I am a big fan of Professor Sean Caroll. (I read all his books). I am also interested in philosophy, and if you want to discuss about consciousness and why humans exist, feel free to drop me a message at howardzhao06@gmail.com.
